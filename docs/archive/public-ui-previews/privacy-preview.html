01

Information We Collect

Personal identification, financial details, and technical device telemetry.
1.1 Account Registration Data

When you register for an account, we collect your verified mobile phone number, full legal name, email address, and optional LINE ID for prize notification delivery.

To process automated deposits and withdrawals, we store masked bank account numbers, PromptPay IDs, and transaction reference codes. We never store raw debit/credit card CVV codes.

Your financial data is processed strictly to credit lottery prize winnings and execute automated bank transfers within our 30-second execution guarantee.
