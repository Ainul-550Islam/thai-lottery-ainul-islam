THE STORY BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Our approach

This platform exists to make lottery results, ticket checking, and account tooling clear and auditable. We publish results with honest source labels, calculate prizes from configured rules, and keep an append-only trail for money movements.

Historical reference is shown as public context. It describes Thailand lottery history, not the founding, ownership, licensing, or legal identity of this platform.

HISTORICAL REFERENCE

Lottery history in Thailand

A concise, source-labelled timeline of milestones described by the observed public reference. History is kept separate from this platform’s independent identity.

01
1874 CE

First recorded lottery reference

The observed reference describes a lottery issued in 1874 during the reign of King Chulalongkorn, connected with a royal birthday celebration and an exhibition.

02
1917 CE

A wartime fundraising lottery

The reference describes a 1917 lottery fundraising context during the First World War.

03
1923 CE

The Tiger Scout Million Baht Lottery

The source describes a 1923 lottery printed in one million copies at one baht each to raise funds for a volunteer organisation.

04
1939 CE

Formal government lottery administration

The source records an administrative transfer to the Ministry of Finance and identifies 5 April 1939 as the founding date of the Government Lottery Office.

05
1974 CE

Statutory public-enterprise status

The source records the 1974 Government Lottery Office Act and its described public-enterprise status.

06
2000 CE

International lottery associations

The observed reference says the Government Lottery Office joined international lottery associations in 2000.
