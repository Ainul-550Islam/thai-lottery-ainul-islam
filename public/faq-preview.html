Account & Identity Verification

Registration, mobile phone OTP, Thai National ID, and KYC requirements.

Click the "Register" button at the top right of the page. Enter your mobile number, verify via 6-digit SMS OTP, choose a secure password, and your account will be active immediately.
You can purchase standard tickets immediately upon SMS verification (Tier 1). However, completing Tier 2 KYC verification (National ID or Passport) is required for withdrawals above 10,000 THB and ensures automated prize payouts.

Deposits, Withdrawals & Banking

PromptPay QR, bank wire transfers, processing speeds, and zero-fee policies.

ThaiLotto supports instant PromptPay QR, all major Thai commercial banks (KBANK, SCB, BBL, KTB, TTB), and USDT TRC-20 crypto stablecoins. Deposits are 100% free with 0.00% platform charge.
PromptPay automated API withdrawals are processed in under 30 seconds, 24/7/365. Standard domestic bank wires clear in under 15 minutes directly to your verified bank account.

Lottery Rules & Betting Mechanics

3D Top (900x), 19 Doors, 3D Tod permutations, and running numbers.

Thai 3-Digit Top pays an industry-leading 900 THB per 1 THB bet (900x odds). For example, a 100 THB straight bet pays exactly 90,000 THB.
19 Doors allows you to select one key digit (0–9), and our system automatically places bets on all 19 two-digit combinations containing that digit (e.g., key 7 generates 07, 17... 70, 71... 79).
